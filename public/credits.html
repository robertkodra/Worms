Small game. Proper credits.

Burrow Brawl is an independent prototype inspired by classic 2D artillery games. It is not affiliated with Team17 or the Worms franchise.

Original prototype work

Character and equipment illustrations, animated battlefield textures, dialogue, effects, and procedural sound were created for this project. Artwork is drawn into local texture canvases and rendered by Three.js. No original game recordings or artwork are included.

Character voices

Thirty original quips were synthesized with CMU Flite 2.2 and its kal voice, then pitch/timing adjusted and peak normalized for this prototype. The speech engine is not included or needed at runtime. See the Flite notices and voice asset record.
