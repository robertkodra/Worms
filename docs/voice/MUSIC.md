# Music handoff

The game is ready to continue without final audio. Send the music files when available; a rough or unmastered export is sufficient for integration work.

Useful first batch:

- One quiet menu loop, approximately 45–90 seconds.
- One light, mischievous battlefield loop, approximately 90–180 seconds. Leave room for short effects and dialogue.
- Optional alternate battlefield loops for Copper Canyon and Frost Hollow.
- Optional short win/loss stingers, 3–6 seconds.

Prefer stereo WAV at 44.1 or 48 kHz, with no clipping, no long leading silence, and a clean loop point. MP3 or Ogg is also usable. Keep an uncompressed master; web derivatives will be prepared and checked after delivery. Add a short note identifying the creator/source and the permission to distribute the music with a public browser game. No personal contact details are needed in public metadata.

The runtime directory is `public/audio/music`. Its manifest is intentionally empty until actual files are integrated and verified; there are no missing-track requests or generated replacement music. Voice lines and their filenames remain in the adjacent recording pack.
